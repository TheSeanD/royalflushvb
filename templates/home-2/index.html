ABOUT COMPANY

Discover Our Journey

10+
Years of Experience

Royal Flush and Back Bay Boxes were founded by brothers Jason and Greg from Pungo, VA, who transitioned from working at the Norfolk Ford plant to running a successful sanitation and dumpster rental business. Over 25 years, they expanded their operations, with Jason specializing in portable toilets and Greg in roll-off dumpsters. In 2024, Bryan and Jameson from Atlantic OBX Inc. acquired both companies, ensuring continued growth and maintaining high standards. Greg remains as Senior Operations Manager, while Jason consults part-time and pursues real estate. Together, they provide exceptional sanitation and waste management solutions.
