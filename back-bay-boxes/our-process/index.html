Our Services

At Royal Flush, we make it easy to get the portable sanitation solutions you need. Our streamlined process ensures that you receive the best service from start to finish.

How we Work

01

Step 1

The first step in our process is to understand your specific needs. Whether you require portable toilets, hand washing stations, or high-end restroom trailers, we’ll help you determine the best options for your event or construction site. Contact us via phone, email, or our online contact form to schedule a consultation. Our experienced team will discuss your requirements, event details, and the number of units needed to provide you with a tailored solution.

02

Step 2

Once we’ve discussed your needs and provided a quote, it’s time to confirm your booking. We’ll finalize the details, including the type and number of units, delivery schedule, and any additional services you may need. You’ll receive a booking confirmation with all the information you need to prepare for the rental period. This step ensures that everything is set for a smooth and hassle-free experience.

03

Step 3

On the day of your event or project, our team will deliver and set up the rented equipment according to the agreed schedule. We pride ourselves on punctuality and professionalism, ensuring that everything is ready before your event starts or your project begins. Throughout the rental period, we offer maintenance and support to keep the units in top condition. After your rental period ends, we’ll handle the pickup and disposal, leaving your site clean and orderly.
